## About Us

The Cherry Bombs was founded in 2025 by Capuchino High School students who noticed a lack of engineering opportunities at their school. Our mission is to give everyone a chance to learn about engineering, communication, and teamwork skills. From a humble handful of inexperienced yet passionate students, we've slowly blossomed into a well sized team.
